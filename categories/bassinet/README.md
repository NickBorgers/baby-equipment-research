# Bassinet

A standalone sleep bassinet for the first months of night sleep: freestanding
or bedside. A bassinet that clips onto a stroller is NOT in this category; it
is a stroller accessory.

## What fits what

A bassinet is checked against the floor space beside the bed and, for
bedside models, against the mattress-top height. See
[`docs/FIT.md`](../../docs/FIT.md).

Our bed is a queen on an upholstered **platform frame that sticks out past
the mattress**, forming a ledge. A bedside bassinet has to sit flush with
the mattress despite that ledge, which is a safety issue, not only a fit
issue. Prefer designs made for platform beds or ledges. Nick's example lead:
the Arm's Reach Versatile Co-Sleeper (not yet evaluated). The ledge geometry
lives in `household.yaml` (`home.bed_ledge_*`, `home.bed_frame_floor_gap_in`).
Open question 19 tracks it.

## Safety notes for whoever picks this up

Safe-sleep claims need primary sources: CPSC, the bassinet standard, AAP
guidance and the manufacturer's manual. Marketing copy for smart bassinets
is not evidence.
