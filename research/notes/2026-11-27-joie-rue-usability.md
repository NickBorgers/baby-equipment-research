Usability pass for the Joie Rue (4-30 lb, under 30 in), against every row
of the car-seat "What to judge" list (open question 36's rule for every

## Findings

All reviews tested the Rue itself, not an older or European Joie seat. No
older Joie seat shares a documented part with the Rue, so no older-seat
findings are used.

### Getting it right every time

**Recline.**

- Manual p.40: "The base has 4 recline positions." "For 0-3 months: Make
  sure the bubble is entirely under the light blue line." A rolled towel is
  allowed.
- Manual p.9 says why recline matters: "An improperly reclined child
  restraint can result in breathing difficulties, injury, or ejection."
  (primary)
- CSFTL expects a wide angle range, "without the need for a rolled towel
  or a pool noodle". That is an expectation, not a sloped-seat test.
- BabyGearLab: "the recline adjustment button is pressed against the
  vehicle seat back when you need it most ... you have to disconnect the
  LATCH connectors to reach the button."
- **Misuse risk:** changing the recline after install means uninstalling
  the base, so a wrong angle may be left in place (BabyGearLab).
- BabyGearLab: the bubble "works well and is easy to see", but "there is a
  narrower window of acceptable angles". It contradicts itself on one or
  two bubbles.
- Reaching the 0-3 month line in a Model Y: not found.

**Inserts and harness fit.**

- Manual p.28: the infant insert "MUST be removed once the child reaches
  11 lb. (5 kg)." The low-birth-weight pillow and head support have no
  weight rule.
- CSFTL, 4 lb / 17 in doll: sliding the bottom fully down put the straps
  "just above the doll's shoulders". Without the newborn cushion the
  harness "was actually not short enough". With no inserts, "this setup was
  the best for our preemie." The shell is "kind of a C-shape, rather than
  an L-shape."
- CSFTL, 7 lb doll: "both options provided a good harness fit."
- **Misuse risk:** sliding the baby fully down, the natural move, puts the
  straps above the shoulders. Some insert combinations leave slack (CSFTL,
  dolls only).
- Safe in the Seat: a 10.5 lb, 23 in 2-month-old "had an excellent fit!"
  (possibly the Canadian version).
- Fit at about 4 and 7 months: not found.
- Harness slots: CSFTL says 4, BabyGearLab 3. Unresolved.

**Base install hardware.**

- Manual: lock-off only. "IMPORTANT: DO NOT over tighten the LATCH strap or
  lock-off will not close" (p.44; same for belt installs).
- **The manual mentions no anti-rebound bar or tensioner.** Joie's support
  page and BabyGearLab say the base has an anti-rebound bar. BabyGearLab
  calls the lock-off a tensioner. No load leg.
- CSFTL: the push-button LATCH strap is threaded so "it's pretty much
  impossible to thread them through the wrong spot."
- BabyGearLab, belt install (4.0/10): hard to get the belt "tight enough,
  but not too tight, so the lockoff wouldn't close"; "more of a twisting
  than most competitors" though it passed the wiggle test.
- **Misuse risk:** the lock-off closes only in a band between too loose
  and too tight (manual, BabyGearLab).
- Whether the lock-off stays locked over time: not found.

**Click-in indicator.** The manual describes no visual indicator, only a
click and a pull-up test (p.56). A false "locked" reading: not found and
not tested. BabyGearLab: "It connects swiftly without any issues."

**Baseless install.**

- Manual p.21/58: "Option 1 (suggested)" is European routing. Option 2 is
  American routing. Lap-only is also allowed.
- CSFTL: "a pretty painless baseless installation."
- BabyGearLab (5.0/10): "we had to use a rolled towel ... It moves around
  a lot after installation."
- The two reviews disagree.

### Which base ships

The Rue's own base (24 x 12.5 x 15 in) and Base AR (23.5 x 15 x 13.25 in)
have different listed sizes. Joie says Base AR fits "any Joie infant car
seat". One owner said the Rue base was discontinued in favor of Base AR
(snippet only). Which base is in the box today: not found.

### US availability

- Babylist's structured data showed six colors "InStock" and Evergreen
  "Discontinued" on 2026-11-27.
- Prices: $169.99 list; $118.99 on Black Friday for some colors.
- Target and Amazon prices appear only as reported by Babylist.
- Joie's support page (updated 2026-09-30) treats the Rue as current.

### Correction to the market survey

The survey listed the Rue's baseless install as American and lap-only. The
manual's suggested method is European routing, with American and lap-only
as options.

## Confidence

- **High** for the manual's rules (recline, 11 lb insert, lock-off, baseless
  routing). A newer printed revision may exist.
- **Medium** for install and daily-friction findings: two hands-on reviews
  of the right model, no retests, and conflicts on baseless stability,
  harness slots and bubbles.
- **Low to medium** for newborn fit (dolls only). Older ages not found.
- **Low** for owner snippets. **Medium** for availability (one retailer's data).
