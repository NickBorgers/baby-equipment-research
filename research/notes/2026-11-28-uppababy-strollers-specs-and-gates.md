# UPPAbaby single strollers: specs and the 0002/0003 gates

## Question

For each current US UPPAbaby single stroller that may take the Aria V2
(ADR 0004): weight, folded and open size, recline, whether the toddler
seat is approved from birth, child limit, fold, wheels, Aria adapter and
price. Then check each against 0003 (newborn-capable on its own, about
50 lb, maker-listed adapter) and 0002 (maker lists the seat).

## Findings

The current US single line is Vista V3, Cruz V3, Kona, Ridge V2 and
Minu V3. The G-Luxe umbrella stroller is also sold. No maker page links
it to an infant seat. No "Minu V4" was found.

### Newborn rule and child limit, from each US manual

- **Minu V3 (250127, NA section):** "This product is suitable from birth
  up to a weight of 50 lbs. (22.7 kg) and height of 40” (101 cm)." "Use
  the most reclined position and the rear crotch buckle slot until the
  baby has strong head and neck control and is able to sit with bended
  knees." The non-NA section of the same manual ("0 to 36 months",
  15 kg) does not apply in the US.
- **Vista V3 (251118, North America section):** "This toddler seat is
  suitable for children from 3 months (from birth with optional Infant
  SnugSeat accessory) until child is 50 lbs (22.7 kg) and child’s height
  is 40” (101 cm)." The product page adds the Bassinet as a second
  from-birth option.
- **Cruz V3 (250711):** "This stroller seat is suitable from birth up to
  50 lbs (22.7 kg) and 40” (101 cm)." "For newborns, use the most
  reclined position of the seat."
- **Kona (260710):** "This stroller seat is suitable from birth up to 50
  lbs (22.7 kg) and 40" (101 cm)." "For newborns, use the most reclined
  position of the seat and foot barrier is in place."
- **Ridge V2 (251216):** "This product is suitable up to a weight of 55
  lbs (25 kg) and height of 40" (101 cm)." "For use from birth with
  Bassinet or UPPAbaby Infant Car Seat." "For use from 3 months with the
  toddler seat."

### Accessories

- SnugSeat: "Not compatible with Cruz V3, which features built-in newborn
  support".
- Bassinet V3: "Transform your Vista, Cruz, Kona, or Ridge into a safe
  and stylish pram". "suitable from birth to 20 lbs or until infant can
  push up on hands and knees, whichever comes first". No Minu bassinet.

### Ridge V2 adapter (updates an earlier note)

The [Aria V2 stroller note](2026-11-28-uppababy-aria-v2-on-stroller-and-model-y.md)
found no page naming an Aria adapter for the Ridge V2. The Ridge V2
product page now lists "Adapters for Ridge (Aria, Mesa & Bassinet)"
(0901-RMA-WW) and says "Compatible from birth with Bassinet, Aria®, and
Mesa® Infant Car Seats with adapters".

### Kona and the Aria V2: the maker disagrees with the seat manual

- Kona page: "Direct attachment for UPPAbaby infant car seats and
  bassinet with no adapters needed".
- Help centre (2026-09-25): "No adaptors are needed for UPPAbaby car
  seats (Aria, Mesa)."
- 2026 comparison PDF: Aria under Kona, "Direct Connection".
- Aria V2 manual (260724) lists only Vista, Cruz, Ridge, Minu and Minu
  Duo. Kona is missing. The Kona manual's car seat steps are pictures
  only.

### Overhead bin (22 × 14 × 9 in)

None of the five fits on published or measured folded size. The Minu V3
is the closest: about 18 in wide against 14, and 9.2-10 in deep against
9. UPPAbaby says the Minu V3 "fits in any overhead bin" and is "IATA
compatible". BGL's table says "Carry-on Compatible? No".

### Minu Duo (double, for the record)

$749.99, 27.4 lb, folded 12.9 × 27.8 × 23.7 in, Aria by adapter. A
double is not needed now.

## Confidence

- **High:** newborn rules, child limits, adapters, maker weights and
  sizes (current US manuals and uppababy.com). Two small maker
  conflicts: Ridge weight (29.2 or 30 lb), Cruz folded height (32.5 or
  32.4 in).
- **Medium:** BGL weights, folded sizes and fold handling. BGL contradicts
  itself on the Cruz weight and wheel material.
- **Medium:** Kona with the Aria V2. Three maker sources say yes; the seat
  manual does not list it.
- **Low:** anything about Kona usability. No hands-on review found.
- Prices are one day's list prices during a sale period.

## Candidate impact

Add five stroller candidates to `candidates/items/stroller.yaml`, status
`candidate`, with the specs above (maker figures; measured figures in
`notes` where they differ):

- `uppababy-minu-v3`, `uppababy-cruz-v3`, `uppababy-kona`: pass 0003 on
  their own.
- `uppababy-vista-v3`: passes 0003 only with an extra SnugSeat or
  Bassinet; record as a con with the price.
- `uppababy-ridge-v2`: its seat is not from birth; newborn use without
  the car seat needs the Bassinet plus adapters. Record as a con. It is
  also the heaviest and largest folded.
- `compat`: Aria V2 direct on Vista V3 and Cruz V3; by adapter on Minu V3
  (0904-MMA-WW) and Ridge V2 (0901-RMA-WW). Kona: leave the Aria V2
  unlisted (unknown) until the seat manual or UPPAbaby confirms, and say
  why in `notes`. `uppababy-mesa-v3` is listed on the same terms.
- `folded_in` is set for all five. None fits the overhead bin; the fit
  report will show it.
