# Primo Viaggio Nido 4-30: were the old problems fixed?

## Question

Follow-up to the [usability note](2026-11-27-peg-perego-primo-viaggio-usability.md).
Nick asked on 2026-11-28 whether the Nido should come back as a front-runner,
and whether the current 4-30 version fixed the old problems. Checked part by
part: click-in indicator, recline, inserts, lock-off, load leg, small-part
friction, carrier weight. Also any hands-on review, NHTSA records and Model Y
reports.

## Findings

**No hands-on test of the 4-30 Nido exists.** Every review is of the 4-35.
The manuals point to the same base as before: the 2025 Nido base drawing
(p. 10) and its base text match the 2020 Lounge 4-35 manual, including
"Red/Green indicator" and "Leg deployment indicator". Drawings are often
reused, so this is not proof that the hardware is unchanged.

**What the 4-30 Nido is (medium, inference).** Peg Perego's 4-30 page lists
SKU IMPV07US00MB53, the MPN Kidsland lists as "4-35 Nido K". Target titles
the same product "4-35 Nido K" with a 4-30 lb spec. So the 4-30 Nido is
probably the Nido K with its label limit cut to 30 lb. Peg Perego never
says so.

| Old problem | Current 4-30 status |
| --- | --- |
| Click-in indicator showed green while unlatched (CSFTL 2017; BabyGearLab 2023 saw none) | **Not fixed, not confirmed.** Same text as 2020: "You will not be able to move the car seat if it is properly locked in place. Check that the red/green indicators … are in the green position" (p. 37). **The manual still does not say to pull up on the handle** to check. |
| Recline by weight zones; newborns may need more | **Unchanged.** p. 27: "set the angle corresponding to the weight of your child." The manual never calls the bubble "just a guide". |
| Recline weight zones | **Not in the manual.** The p. 27 drawing has no zones or numbers; the zones are on a label on the base. **Correction:** the 2026-11-27 note said they were "in an image" in the manual. The 4-30 zones are not found. |
| Recline out of reach in some cars | **Unchanged.** The Nido manual allows a towel or pool noodle only for the baseless install (p. 39). The 2025 Urban Mobility manual, with the same base, allows "a rolled towel or pool noodle under the base". The conflict stands. CR's con is unchanged. |
| Insert fit; when to remove stage 1 | **Rule still unclear.** p. 52: the dual-stage system "may be used from birth up to about 20 lbs (9 kg) or when the torso … fully occupies the space". No separate stage-1 rule. The 2017 Nido manual said stage 1 "until around 8 pounds" (quoted by CSFTL); the 2025 Lounge still says about 8 lb. Whether the inserts are new parts: not found. |
| Lock-off popped open (BabyGearLab 2023; disputed) | **No change shown.** Same "Right Tight lockoff" name and drawing as 2020. No test of the 4-30. |
| Load leg awkward; no floor-contact indicator | **Still no floor-contact indicator.** The leg indicator shows only that the leg is deployed; p. 30: "The correct adjustment of the foot is when the foot touches the floor". The 2025 manual now says "Always deploy" the leg; the 2020 Lounge said it could be used with or without. |
| Hard release from base | One Target owner, 2026-11-12, 4-30 listing: "Got stuck to the base"; "the latch system is broken on one side" after about five months. Single report; which latch is unclear. |
| Stiff buckle, flimsy LATCH door | Not found for the 4-30. |
| Carrier weight as used | Not found for the 4-30. Maker: 8.2 lb "without hood and inserts". 4-35 measured: 10.6 lb (BabyGearLab), 11 lb with inserts (CSFTL, Car Crash Detective). |

**A second case of maker advice against its manual.** NHTSA complaint
10950369 (2017, older 4-35 base) quotes a Peg Perego CPST advising to
"complete the install so that the child is correctly positioned… and
disregard the bubble." One complaint, different base, low confidence.

**NHTSA (primary):** no ease-of-use rating for any Primo Viaggio. The NIDO
record (id 117904) and the "4/35 NIDO" records show 0 complaints, 0 recalls,
0 investigations. No record names IMPV07, Nido K or a 4-30 model.

**Model Y:** not found.

## Confidence

- **High** for what the 2025 manuals say, the absence of zones in the Nido
  manual, and the NHTSA records.
- **Medium** that the 4-30 Nido is the relabelled Nido K, and that the base
  parts are described the same since 2020.
- **Low** for whether any usability problem exists or is gone on the 4-30:
  there is no hands-on evidence.

## Candidate impact

Nothing. The Primo Viaggio has no candidate entry and stays off the
shortlist. Nothing found fixes the reasons it dropped (recline reachability
with no fix under the base, the click-in indicator, the carrier weight, no
review of the current version). The click-in risk is handled by pulling up
on the handle every time, which the manual does not tell owners to do.
