# Non-UPPAbaby strollers that take the Aria V2

## Question

Which strollers not made by UPPAbaby take the Aria V2 (US 1002-ARI),
according to the stroller maker's or adapter maker's own chart, manual or
adapter page? For each: 0003 gate (a), newborn use without the car seat,
and gate (b), a seat rated to about 50 lb. Maker weight and folded size
where the page gives them.

## Findings

### Which makers name the Aria V2

| Maker | What it names | List date |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Mockingbird | "Aria V2" and "Aria" (V1) | November 2026 |
| Zoe | "Aria V2" only | undated |
| Thule | "Aria" | June 2025 |
| Veer | "Aria" | November 2024 |
| BOB | "MESA" only | January 2025 |
| Bumbleride | no UPPAbaby adapter | 2026-03-23 |

The Aria V2 launched by October 2025 (Strolleria spotlight 2025-10-09, in
the [Aria V2 stroller note](2026-11-28-uppababy-aria-v2-on-stroller-and-model-y.md)).
The Thule and Veer lists are older. Inference: their "Aria" means the V1,
so the V2 is unknown on them. BOB is unknown for any Aria.

### Strollers that name the V2

All figures are maker-stated; nothing was measured.

The Single-to-Double 3.0 uses the same adapter; it is 50 lb single, 45 lb
per seat as a double, about 27 lb single, folded 34 × 25.5 × 18 in. The
UPPAbaby adapter is "not compatible for twin configurations". A double is
not needed now, so it is not a candidate.

### Quotes

- Mockingbird chart image, UPPAbaby box: "Aria\* / Aria V2\* / Mesa -
  2020 model or newer / Mesa V2 / Mesa V3 / Mesa Max\*"; "\*Not compatible
  for twin configurations".
- Mockingbird help: "If your exact car seat model name is not listed in
  this chart, it is not compatible with the Mockingbird Car Seat
  Adapter."
- Mockingbird: QuickConnect is "Not compatible with Single Stroller"; the
  full-size adapter is "Not compatible with Travel Stroller".
- Mockingbird Travel Stroller: "Infant Nest Mode ... With a lie-flat
  recline and built-in UPF 50+ leg cover"; "Maximum weight: 50 lbs";
  "Compact fold: 21.5" tall x 18" wide x 10" deep (IATA-compliant to fit
  in overhead bin)"; "Lightweight: 17.7 lbs".
- Mockingbird Single 3.0: "Minimum age: 3 months, or use from birth with
  Mockingbird's Infant Seat Insert, Bassinet or Car Seat Adapter (all sold
  separately)". A "w/ Newborn Insert" variant costs $520, against $475.
- Zoe chart, Tourᵛ³ column: "Mesa V2 Mesa V3 Aria V2". Adapter page:
  "Not compatible with The Tourᵛ² or The Tandemᵛ²".
- Zoe Tour V3: "With a 155° recline and an included wrap-around cocoon,
  the seat is newborn-compatible without additional accessories."
  "Carrying Capacity 60 lbs"; "Net Weight 14.5 lbs".
- Thule UG3 chart: "Uppababy / Mesa V2 / Mesa Max / Aria"; "Approved up
  to 9 kg / 20 lbs (child weight)".
- Veer: "&Roll: Mesa, Mesa V2, Mesa Max, Aria".
- BOB: "Compatible UPPAbaby Infant Car Seats: MESA".
- Bumbleride: "We do not currently have an adapter that will allow any of
  our strollers to accommodate a UppaBaby car seat or bassinet."

### Not candidates, and why

- **Thule Urban Glide 3:** V2 unknown; jogger; newborn mode needs a
  $299.95 bassinet; the adapter is approved only to a 20 lb child, so the
  adapter would end car seat use before the Aria's 30 lb limit
  (inference).
- **Veer Switch&Roll:** V2 unknown; newborn mode needs an accessory.
- **BOB:** Aria unknown (Mesa only); Strolleria lists BOB as Aria
  compatible, but BOB's own page does not.

## Confidence

- **High** for the Aria version each maker names, adapter names, and
  maker limits, weights and folded sizes. Current maker pages, read
  today. The Mockingbird charts are images, but match the page text.
- **Medium** for Zoe's newborn mode: "near flat" at 155° is the maker's
  wording, not a measurement.
- **Medium** for "not found" at Bugaboo, Joolz, Stokke, Colugo, Larktale
  and Bumbleride; **low** at Nuna, Baby Jogger, Cybex, Silver Cross US,
  Wonderfold, Graco and Evenflo (site maps and snippets only).

## Candidate impact

Add three candidates to `candidates/items/stroller.yaml`, status
`candidate`:

- `mockingbird-travel-stroller`: passes 0003 with no add-on; Aria V2 by
  included FlexConnect adapter or $50 QuickConnect.
- `zoe-tour-v3`: passes 0003 with no add-on; Aria V2 by $55 adapter.
- `mockingbird-single-3`: passes 0003 only with an extra newborn
  accessory; Aria V2 by $50 adapter.

22 × 14 × 9 in bin on published size, despite the makers' bin claims.
