# Consumer Reports ratings for the shortlisted infant seats

## Question

What do Consumer Reports' member pages say about the Aria V2, Mesa V3 and
KeyFit 30 ClearTex: scores, pros and cons, and tested version? Where do
they agree or conflict with the existing notes? The Primo Viaggio Nido is
included for comparison only.

## Findings

### CR has not tested the versions we would buy

- **Aria V2: no CR page.** The only Aria page is V1. It was published
  before V2 came out, and it mentions a "load leg indicator", which V2
  dropped.
- **Mesa V3: no CR page.** The only Mesa is the Mesa V2.
- **KeyFit 30: version unknown.** The page dates from 2009 and names no
  model number, test date or 213a claim. Whether CR tested a 213a-labeled
  unit is not found.
- The 2025-12-17 score date is on every page, so it looks like a rescore
  of the whole category, not a retest (inference).
- CR's crash rating covers simulated 35 mph frontal crashes only. CR says
  its tests "are not designed to check whether a seat complies with
  current federal car seat safety standards". It says nothing about 213a.

Scores on the list run from 35 to 88.

### Pros and cons (verbatim)

- **Aria (V1).** Pros: "Lightweight carrier"; "Push-on LATCH connectors
  with automatic retractors that improve installation"; "Tightness
  indicator ensures secure base installation"; "No-rethread harness".
  Cons: "Seat belt can be tricky to keep tight while closing the lock
  off"; "Carry handle must be in one position during car travel" (V2
  changed this). Take: "scored excellent lower anchor installations in
  our evaluations."
- **Mesa V2.** Pros include "Tightness indicator ensures secure base
  installation" and "Excellent labels and instructions". Cons: "Can be
  difficult to hold the needed tension on seat belt while closing the
  lock off"; "Handle position is restricted in the vehicle".
- **KeyFit 30.** Pros include "Push-on LATCH connectors make LATCH
  installation easy", "Single-pull LATCH tension is easy to adjust",
  "Carrier locks easily into base", "Handle can be in any locked position
  during travel" and "Easy-to-use level indicator on base". Cons: "Recline
  indicator not labeled"; "Harness adjustment requires rethreading".
- **Nido.** Con: "Insufficient recline adjustment for newborns in some
  vehicles". Take still says "4-35 Nido" on a 4-30 page.

### Carrier weights (CR "to the nearest pound", method not stated)

Aria V1 7 lb; KeyFit 30 9 lb; Nido 10 lb; Mesa V2 11 lb.

### Agreement and conflict with the existing notes

## Confidence

- **Medium** for CR's results on the versions it tested: Aria V1, Mesa V2,
  and a KeyFit 30 of unknown version.
- **Low** for applying them to the Aria V2, Mesa V3 or a 213a-labeled
  KeyFit 30.
- **High** that CR has no Aria V2 or Mesa V3 page, and for the ranks.

## Candidate impact

- **All three:** add this note to `research_notes`.
- **`uppababy-aria-v2`:** tightness-indicator con: add that CR (V1) lists
  the indicator as a pro, so the labs conflict. Notes: CR has not tested
  V2; V1 scored 78.
- **`uppababy-mesa-v3`:** notes: CR has not tested V3; the Mesa V2 scored
  68 and is not CR Recommended.
- **`chicco-keyfit-30-cleartex`:** pro: CR overall 86, 6th of 46, with
  5/5 LATCH and belt fit; the tested version is unknown.
