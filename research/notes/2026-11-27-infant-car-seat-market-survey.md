# Infant car seat market survey against 0001 and 0002

## Question

Decision [0001](../../decisions/0001-car-seat-must-meet-fmvss-213a.md)
requires an explicit FMVSS 213a claim from the maker. Decision
[0002](../../decisions/0002-infant-car-seat-that-clicks-onto-the-stroller.md)
lists the comparison criteria: carrier weight, carrier width against the
FAA's 16 in guide, aircraft install, fit in the Model Y's outboard seats, and
the range of strollers the seat clicks onto. Which infant seats on the US
market qualify, and how do they compare?

This builds on the
[first 213a pass](2026-11-27-fmvss-213a-status-and-infant-seats.md). It
re-reads the claims for seats worth shortlisting and fills the Joie gap.
Rotating seats are covered in a
[separate note](2026-11-27-rotating-infant-car-seats.md).

Notes on the table:

- **All of them are wider than the FAA's 16 in guide**, Peg Perego
  included (17-17.25 in). The narrowest is the KeyFit 30 at 16.75 in. Every candidate will need the airline seat
  width checked (open question 22 follow-up), or a seat with movable
  armrests (AC 120-87C para 10f(1)).
- **Aria V2 has the lightest carrier (6 lb)**, but that figure leaves out
  the canopy and insert; the others' figures do not say. Its manual allows
  only European routing for a baseless car install
  ("There is only 1 method of carrier-only installation. LAP/SHOULDER EU
  ROUTING METHOD", re-read in the manual). That needs a lap/shoulder belt
  long enough to wrap the back of the carrier, which taxis abroad usually
  have but not always.
- **The Chicco 213a claims are on the product pages only.** The KeyFit 30
  manual is dated 03-2020, before 213a. Chicco's blog says all seats now
  in production meet 213a and 213b (first note).
- **Doona+ is the weakest of the explicit group**: the claim is on the
  safety page, not in the manual. Doona X says it in the manual.
- **Joie availability is unclear.** The Rue, Mint Latch and Rue LX Spin
  pages each contain a "This item is coming soon" notify form; it could not
  be told whether this was shown or hidden template text. Rue is listed at
  $118.99 sale price. Treat Joie availability as unknown.
- Joie's Rue LX Spin page names the "Baby Safety Alliance" as a certifier.
  That is the name of the group whose petition is pending in the 213a
  docket (open question 24).
- The Doona X LATCH base forbids its rigid LATCH install over 25 lb
  (base guide p.5); above that it is belt-installed.

### Peg Perego Primo Viaggio (explicit 213a, in the manual)

All four current US variants carry the same "Declaration of conformity"
in their 2025 manuals: "Reference Standards (origin) FMVSS 571-213 /
FMVSS 571-213 a / FMVSS 571-213 b ... has been certified by external
independent laboratories, in compliance with current legislation" (Nido
p.63 and Urban Mobility, re-read). Shared facts:

- 4-30 lb, maximum height 32 in; outgrow when the head is less than 1 in
  below the top of the seat back.
- "This unit is certified for aircraft use." Lap belt through the slots on
  the handle; "Do not use the base to install the restraint on an
  aircraft."
- Baseless car install: European routing ("Pull the shoulder portion of the
  belt around the back of the seat"), or lap portion only. The handle in its
  forward position acts as an anti-rebound bar without the base.
- Expiry: "seven (7) years from the date of purchase".
- **Strollers: the widest chart of any qualifying seat.** Peg Perego's own
  list (July 2026) shows Urban Mobility, Nido and Lounge on BOB, Bugaboo
  (Butterfly, Dragonfly, Donkey, Fox 5), Bumbleride, Cybex (Balios, Gazel,
  Mios, Priam), Inglesina, Joolz (Dot, Hub2), Silver Cross, Thule, UPPAbaby
  (Cruz V3, Minu V3, Ridge V2, Vista V3), Valco, Veer and Wonderfold. The
  UPPAbaby strollers use Peg's "Adapter/Links for UPPAbaby" ($49.99);
  the others use the stroller maker's Maxi-Cosi adapter plus Peg's
  "Adapter/Links Universal" ($49.99). Peg Perego strollers use a $19.99
  link.
- Extra base with load leg and anti-rebound bar: $149.99.
- No NHTSA recall records found for Peg Perego in the 2010-onward file.

### Legacy or discontinued variants (no 213a claim, avoid old stock)

Chicco KeyFit 35 (4-35 lb), KeyFit 30 Zip / Zip Air, Fit2 / Fit2 Adapt
(4-35 lb) and the 22 lb KeyFit ClearTex / Encore: no 213a text on their

### Borderline (names 213a or a new side-impact test without a "meets/certified" claim)

- **Graco** (SnugRide Lite LX, SnugFit 35 LX, other Graco seats): "Side-impact
  tested according to FMVSS 213a with the built-in 5-point harness system"
  (gracobaby.com, via search snippets only; site 403).

- **Britax Willow S** (4-30 lb, 9.2 lb carrier, 16.9 in wide, $279.99):
  "side-impact tested according to FMVSS 213a and meets FMVSS 213 frontal
  impact standards" (page only; the manual does not say it). Carrier-only
  on aircraft; European baseless routing allowed; Alpine ClickTight base
  with anti-rebound bar, no load leg. Strollers per Britax's chart: Brook,
  Brook+, Grove direct; adapters for Juniper+, BOB; UPPAbaby Vista V2.
- **Cybex Aton G2 / Aton G2 Swivel / Cloud G Pro** (4-30 lb): pages say

  the Aton G2 Swivel and Cloud G Pro manuals (ManualsLib copies) say
  "meets all applicable requirements of ... (FMVSS) 213b". 213b implies
  213a by inference only. Aton G2: 9 lb carrier, 17.5 in wide, load-leg
  base, $399.95, all Cybex strollers. Cloud G Pro: 12 lb carrier, rigid
  LATCH, load leg and anti-rebound bar, $549.95. Cybex's own safety FAQ

- **Cybex Cloud T** (US, $649.95): FAQ "complies with the new FMVSS 213
  side impact and frontal impact safety standards"; the only manual found

### Model Y fit

The only independent test of an infant seat in a pre-refresh Model Y is
Cars.com's, with a Chicco KeyFit 30: grade A, it "fit easily behind the
front passenger seat". No test was found for any other seat here. The
2022.40 Tesla manual (hosted copy, see the
[Model Y note](2026-11-27-model-y-2022-cargo-and-rear-seat.md)) says
nothing about load legs (support legs), so whether the rear floor suits
a load leg is unknown.

## Confidence

- **High** for the UPPAbaby, Chicco, Doona, Joie and Peg Perego wording,
  limits, weights, widths and aircraft rules: read on the makers' pages and
  manuals. The Aria V2 baseless-install rule and the Peg Perego 213a
  declaration were re-read in the manuals.
