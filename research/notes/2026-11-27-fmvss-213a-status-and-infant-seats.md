# FMVSS 213a: status, how to verify it, and which infant seats claim it

## Question

Decision [0001](../../decisions/0001-car-seat-must-meet-fmvss-213a.md)
requires a car seat certified to FMVSS 213a. When does the rule take
effect? Can stores still sell seats made before that? How do you tell
whether a particular seat is certified? Which rear-facing infant seats
explicitly claim 213a today?

## Findings

### The date

The compliance date was delayed twice:

- The 2022 rule set it at 2025-06-30.
- NHTSA proposed a delay in May 2025. In July 2025 it paused enforcement,
  and said that manufacturers "should not market or otherwise describe their
  products as meeting FMVSS No. 213a unless they are certifying to the
  standard."
- The final rule of 2026-09-09 says: "The revised compliance date for FMVSS
  No. 213a is December 5, 2026." Early compliance is allowed. The delay was to
  let "small manufacturers... complete validation and conformance testing at
  oversubscribed third-party test facilities". Graco and Dorel opposed it.

A petition for reconsideration from the "Baby Safety Alliance" (2026-10-23)
was logged in the docket on 2026-11-24. What it asks for is unknown, because
the PDF could not be fetched.

### What it requires

213a applies to add-on car seats for children up to 40 lb or 1100 mm,
which includes every rear-facing infant seat. The test is "a dynamic sled
test simulating a full-scale vehicle-to-vehicle side impact... simulating
both an intruding door and a longitudinal crash component" (2022 preamble).
Seats rated up to 30 lb are tested with a 12-month-old dummy (CRABI).
Seats rated over 30 lb must also pass with a 3-year-old dummy (Q3s).

### Seats made before the date

Under 49 U.S.C. 30112, the rule applies by **date of manufacture**, not
date of sale. A seat made before 2026-12-05 can lawfully be sold afterward,
uncertified.

The required labels include "Manufactured in ____" (month and year) and a
generic statement that the seat "conforms to all applicable Federal motor
vehicle safety standards". **No label wording specific to 213a was found.**
So on a seat made before the date, the generic statement does not prove 213a.

### How to verify a specific seat

1. A seat **manufactured on or after 2026-12-05** must be certified to 213a.
2. A seat made earlier needs the manufacturer's explicit statement for that
   model and production period. Since NHTSA's July 2025 notice, an explicit
   "meets FMVSS 213a" claim should mean the maker is certifying.
3. Inference, not a sourced fact: the 213b standard (S5(b)(2)) requires
   seats certified to 213b to also meet 213a. So "certified to 213b" implies
   213a.

**Warning sign:** Graco told NHTSA that 213a-compliant rear-facing-only
seats have moved to a 30 lb maximum, because a higher rating triggers the
3-year-old dummy test. NHTSA notes the standard does not forbid infant seats
over 30 lb. So a 35 lb infant seat is a warning sign, not proof. Chicco
shows the pattern: KeyFit 30 claims 213a; KeyFit 35 does not.

## Confidence

- **High** for the rule history, the compliance date and the sale-by-
  manufacture-date point. These come from the Federal Register and eCFR.
- **High** for the UPPAbaby, Chicco, Doona, Cybex, Britax and Nuna wording,
  read on the makers' sites.
- **Low** for Joie, Graco, Maxi-Cosi, Evenflo and Clek Liing, which come from
  snippets or retailer copy.
- Manufacturers change their pages. Re-check before buying.

## Candidate impact

- **Under decision 0001, the clearly qualifying infant seats today:**
  UPPAbaby Mesa V3, UPPAbaby Aria V2, Chicco KeyFit 30 and KeyFit Max
  variants, and Doona +. Joie Mint Latch/Rue would qualify once its wording
  is confirmed on joiebaby.com.
- **Borderline, which needs a ruling from Nick or the maker:** Cybex
  Aton G2 ("complies with new FMVSS213 side impact"), and Britax Willow S and
  Graco SnugRide ("tested according to 213a"). A rule for these is open
  question 25.
- **Not qualifying on current evidence:** Nuna PIPA family, Maxi-Cosi
  Peri/Mico, Chicco KeyFit 35, Clek Liingo, Bugaboo Turtle Air.
- When a car seat candidate is added, record "FMVSS 213a" in
  `safety.standards` only for the explicit-claim group, with this note as
  the source. Record the manufacture-date check at purchase in `notes`.
- After 2026-12-05, a seat with a manufacture date on or after that day is
  certified to 213a regardless of marketing. That makes the in-store check
  simpler: read the "Manufactured in" label.
