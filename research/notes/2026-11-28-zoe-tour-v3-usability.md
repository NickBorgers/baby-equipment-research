## Findings

### Price

"Regular price $299", seen 2026-11-28, with 15% off at checkout until
2026-11-30. Adapter $55 (model 810033595783). Carry straps are a $20
extra. Black showed both "Add to cart" and "Notify Me When Available".
Warranty: "2 years-post purchase … accessories are covered for 1 year".

### What it is (maker figures; nothing measured)

- 14.5 lb ("Net Weight"); the same page also says "Only 13 pounds" and
  the FAQ "only 14lbs!". As-used weight: not found.
- Open: 41.5 H × 25.5 D × **21.75 W** in. Folded with belly bar: 10 × 32
  × 21.75 in.
- Basket: "Do not load more than 10 lbs. (4.5kg) in the basket".
- Handlebar: three heights, 38.5, 40, 41.5 in. Wheels: 6 in front, 6¼ in
  rear.

### Newborn use without the car seat

- Manual: "Recline seat to the lowest position before placing newborn
  inside … Locate the infant wrap underneath the calf rest and use the
  Velcro straps to wrap around the sides of the stroller frame."
- Manual warning: "NEVER use in reclined carriage position unless the
  front cover is wrapped around the bumper bar and securely fastened."
  The manual names the frame in one place and the bumper bar in another.
- Recline 155°, "Near flat" (maker). Stroller Mom repeats "near-flat" and
  did not use it with a newborn. The Tour V2 had no newborn mode, so V2
  reviews do not apply here.

### The Aria V2 on the Tour V3 adapter

From the adapter sheet:

- "recline the seat back. Then, slide the adapters onto the connection
  points until you hear an audible click. … Pull up on the adapters to
  ensure they are locked in place."
- "Place the car seat into the car seat adapters and gently, but firmly
  press down until you hear an audible click. Pull up on the car seat
  carrying handle to ensure each side of the car seat is safely locked
  into place before stroller use."
- "we also recommend removing the canopy on the side of the stroller you
  will be using the car seat."
- "Do not use when a child is in the stroller seat. … Always remove
  adapter before folding the stroller."
- No weight limit given. The stroller manual has no car seat section.

How you know it is locked: a click at each stage and a pull-up check; no
visual indicator. **No reviewer tested this adapter or any UPPAbaby seat
on the Tour V3.** Stability with a carrier and carrier height: not found.

### Brakes

Manual: "Step on the rear brake lever on each side of the stroller." Two
pedals. Stroller Mom (V3) con: "Dual-action brake (requires pressing two
pedals to lock fully)". One V3 owner: "I wish the brake was just one bar
across instead of two. I also feel like the clip doesn't keep it closed".
Lucie's List (V2): "I only pushed them halfway and the stroller began to
roll".

### Fold and carry

Manual: lock the brakes, recline the seat, close the canopy, "Pull upward
on the strap … as it folds in half. Lock the fold latch". Stroller Mom
(V3): "Easy one-hand fold"; cons: "Does not stand on its own when folded";
the carry strap is a separate purchase.

### Flying

Zoe pages disagree: the adapter FAQ says "fits in some overhead bins … You
can gate check The Tour at the end of the jet bridge"; the Tour V3 FAQ says
"will fit in MOST airline overhead compartments". Stroller Mom: "plan to
gate-check the Tour". No V3 flight or durability report found.

### Push, canopy, basket

- **Manual: "Do not use the stroller on steep slopes, stairs, escalator,
  beaches, rough roads, mud etc."**
- Stroller Mom (V3): "I found the push nice with my kids". No terrain
  detail. Lucie's List (V2): fine on pavement, packed dirt and grass, not
  loose gravel. Curbs: not found.
- Canopy: "Large, extendable canopy with great sun coverage" (Stroller
  Mom), but it may need to come off one side with the car seat on. Basket:
  a large diaper bag fit (Stroller Mom).

### Misuse risks

- Misuse risk: the two-pedal brake can be half-set or set on one side only
  (one V3 review, one V3 owner; V2 rolled when half-set).
- Misuse risk: the newborn wrap must be fastened, or the leg-opening
  strangulation warning applies; the manual is unclear where it fastens.
- Misuse risk: the adapter must come off before folding and must not be
  used with a child in the seat; each step is easy to skip (inference).
- The manual rules out "rough roads", which conflicts with daily walks on
  rough ground.

### Also noticed

Consumer Reports rated the Zoe **Traveler** (a different model) "Do Not
Buy" on 2026-02-11: it "tipped backward at less than 12 degrees" reclined
and loaded to its 45 lb maximum. Zoe "categorically disagree[s]". The Tour
V3 was not tested (read through Nick's CR account). Inference only: a
stability test of the Tour V3 would be worth finding.
