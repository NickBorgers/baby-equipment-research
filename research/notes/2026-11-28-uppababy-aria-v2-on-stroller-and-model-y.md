## Findings

### Which strollers, which adapters

Aria V2 manual p. 67: "Compatible with Vista V3, Vista V2, Vista 2015+,
Cruz V3, Cruz V2, Cruz. Compatible with Ridge, Minu and Minu Duo strollers
with use of adapters specific to each model."

| Stroller | Adapter for the Aria |
| --- | --- |
| Vista V3 / V2 / 2015+ | None ("No adapters necessary!", Vista guide) |
| Vista, lower seat in double mode | Lower Adapters 0901-VLA-NA |
| Cruz V3 / V2 / Cruz | None |
| Minu V3 | 0904-MMA-WW |
| Minu / Minu V2, Minu Duo | Separate model-specific adapters |
| Ridge | 0901-RMA-WW. No page names an adapter for the Ridge V2 |

The adapter pages say "Aria", never "Aria V2". The V2 manual is what puts
the V2 on these strollers. The Minu V3 + Aria V2 bundle page contradicts
itself: it says the Aria V2 "connects directly to the Minu V3", and later
that it attaches "with the addition of convenient adapters".

### Required positions

- Aria V2 manual p. 67: "With carrier facing stroller handle and carrier
  handle in carry position, align carrier handle hubs with attachment
  posts on stroller." "Rock stop position is not permitted while using the
  stroller." Check by "Lift up on car seat handle".
- "Prior to fitting Aria carrier to stroller frame, engage brake on
  stroller and remove toddler seat from frame."
- Minu V3 adapter page: "Recline seat fully to allow for optimal car seat
  attachment."

### Height from the ground: not found

No maker or reviewer gives a ground-to-carrier height for the V1 or V2 on
any UPPAbaby stroller. The only figure is relative: the Cruz V2/Kona upper
adapter is "Designed to elevate the UPPAbaby Mesa or Aria Infant Car
Seat … by 4”". It is listed for the Cruz V2 and Kona, not the Cruz V3.

### Load limits and stability warnings

| Stroller (rev.) | Basket | Instability wording |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Minu V3 (250127) | 20 lb | "Excessive weight on the stroller may cause a hazardous unstable condition to exist." "When using accessories that attach to stroller handle, always have toddler seat in the upright position and infant car seat parent facing." "only attach one weight bearing accessory to the stroller handle bar at a time." |
| Vista V3 (251118) | 30 lb | "Unit may become unstable if manufacturer’s recommended load is exceeded, or if parcel bag or accessory … is used or hung from the unit." |
| Cruz V3 (250711) | 30 lb | "Parcels or accessory items, or both, placed on the unit may cause the unit to become unstable." |
| Ridge V2 (251216) | 20 lb | "Excessive weight on the stroller may cause a hazardous unstable condition to exist." |

No manual gives a handlebar load limit in pounds. The car seat attachment
steps are pictures only, so text search cannot read them.

**Tip-over reports: none found** for any Aria and UPPAbaby pairing.
Reddit was blocked, so this is low confidence.

### Model Y with an Aria: not found

- No install report for an Aria V1 or V2 in any Model Y, with or without
  the base. Tried TMC, Reddit (snippets only), Cars.com, CSFTL, Car Seat
  Lady.
- Nearest (TMC, 2025-02-22, Model S and new Model 3, version not stated):
  "Infant seat fit through the door opening on both cars without issue";
  "Plenty of front leg room on the S, good enough on the 3."
- Aria V2 manual on the load leg: "We recommend removing the vehicle floor
  mat before installation." "Ensure good contact between load leg foot and
  floor."
- A TikTok titled "Uppababy Aria Carseat Tesla Model Y" exists (snippet
  only); its content could not be read.

## Confidence

- **High** for adapters, required positions, basket limits and warning
  wording (current US manuals and maker pages).
- **Medium** for the 4 in lift of the Cruz upper adapter (maker page
  showing "Notify Me", Cruz V2 and Kona only).
- **Low** for "no tip-over reports" and for everything about the Model Y.
