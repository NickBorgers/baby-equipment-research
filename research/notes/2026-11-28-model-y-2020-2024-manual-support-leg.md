# Model Y 2020-2024 US manual: support legs

## Question

The [Model Y fit note](2026-11-27-model-y-infant-seat-fit-and-load-leg.md)
found support-leg wording only in a 2022.16 Japan edition, and asked for a
North American manual to confirm it. Does the current US 2020-2024 manual
mention support legs (load legs) or the second-row floor?

## Findings

- **The US 2020-2024 manual does not mention support legs.** The full text
  has no "support leg", "load leg" or "safety leg". The live Child Safety
  Seats page agrees.
- **It does not describe a second-row floor compartment.** Interior Storage
  (pp. 32-33) lists consoles, cup holders, hangers and the glovebox. The
  only underfloor storage is "the lower trunk compartment". The floor-mat
  warning (p. 207) is about the driver's mat and the pedals.
- **What it does say about our install:** "Lower LATCH anchors are provided
  in the second row outboard seats." "For the second row, install LATCH
  child safety seats in the outboard seating positions only." Its own
  check: "If the seat moves more than 1 in. (2.5 cm), it is too loose."
- **The support-leg wording is in the 2025+ manual, not the 2020-2024
  one.** The AU page (tagged `model_y_2025`): "All passenger seats in
  Model Y support the use of a child restraint system with a safety leg.
  If the child restraint system is equipped with a leg, extend the leg as
  described in the instructions provided by the manufacturer of the child
  restraint system." This matches the 2022.16 Japan edition wording in the
  earlier note.
- **Not found:** an AU or EU page for the 2020-2024 car. tesla.com blocked
  direct reads by curl and WebFetch (403); no archived copy was found.

## Confidence

- **High** that the US 2020-2024 manual (software 2026.32) says nothing
  about support legs or the second-row floor.
- **High** for the AU 2025+ wording.
- **Low** for whether 2020-2024 cars outside North America carry it; only
  the 2022.16 Japan edition shows it.

## Candidate impact

Nothing changes. Silence is not a ban. The load-leg question was settled
on 2026-11-27 by Nick's confirmation that the floor is a solid plate. The
seat makers' manuals govern load-leg use.
