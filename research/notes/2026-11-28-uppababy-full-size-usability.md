stroller from birth, for walks and gate-checked flights, with the Aria V2.

## Findings

### Part A: the Aria V2 on the Kona

| Source | Says |
| --- | --- |
| Aria V2 manual 260724 (newest), p. 67 | "Compatible with Vista V3, Vista V2, Vista 2015+, Cruz V3, Cruz V2, Cruz. Compatible with Ridge, Minu and Minu Duo strollers with use of adapters specific to each model." No Kona. |
| Kona manual 260710 | No car seat section. The picture pages cover wheels, brake, bumper bar, seat, handlebar, harness, recline, canopy, newborn mode, fold and fabrics. **No figure shows an infant seat.** |
| Kona + Aria V2 bundle ($1,049.98) | "The Aria V2 Car Seat attaches directly to the Kona Stroller". "Connect From Day One." |
| Kona page | "Direct attachment for UPPAbaby infant car seats and bassinet with no adapters needed". |
| Help centre 43763742563607 (2026-09-25) | "No adaptors are needed for UPPAbaby car seats (Aria, Mesa)." |
| Help centre 43763238633367 (2026-09-25) | "compatible out of the box with our bassinet and infant car seats (no adaptors needed)." |
| Help centre 21520031651351 (2026-03-31), 40280628103703 (2026-05-06) | Vista and Cruz only. Both predate the Kona's launch. |

So the maker sells the Kona with the Aria V2 as a bundle and says it
attaches directly. No manual describes the attachment or its check.

The bundle page also says "the carrier can reverse to an outward-facing
position". The Aria V2 manual gives only "With carrier facing stroller
handle", and "Rock stop position is not permitted while using the
stroller."

The Upper Adapters 0901-CUA-WW are sold "for Cruz V2, Kona" (not the Cruz
V3). UPPAbaby publishes no statement that the Kona shares a frame, wheels
or seat with the Cruz V3. Babylist: the Kona's "suspension is good, but
not great. (The Vista and the Cruz, for example, offer much smoother
rides.)"

### Part B: the car seat on the stroller (all three)

**No hands-on source tested any Aria or Mesa on any of the three.** Lock
feel, carrier height, stability with the carrier, and basket access with
the carrier on are all unknown. CR's ease-of-use score includes car seat
installation on compatible strollers, but CR does not name the seat.

The lock check (Aria V2 manual): "An audible click indicates proper
attachment. Lift up on car seat handle to check attachment." There is no
visual indicator. The toddler seat comes off the frame before the carrier
goes on, so it must be stored or carried (inference from the manual).

### Cruz V3

- **Weight and fold:** 26.5 lb measured, folded 22.5 × 17.5 × 33 in
  (BabyGearLab); CR 26 lb. The Bump: a "tight squeeze to get in alone in
  the back of our SUV"; "awkward to carry, even when folded, since there's
  no clear place to lift the stroller". BabyGearLab: fits "most trunks with
  ease".
- **Newborn:** The Bump: "Pull up and fasten the newborn apron, add the
  infant insert, and the reclined seat forms a nest-like structure",
  "effective enough to skip the UPPAbaby Bassinet V3". BabyGearLab:
  "reclines almost completely flat". CR: "lay-flat seat with an integrated
  foot barrier".
- **Brakes:** single action; two colour-coded pedals show the state at a
  glance (BabyGearLab). CR safety 4/5, which includes CR's own stability
  and brake tests.
- **Fold:** "requires two hands, auto-locks, and self-stands"
  (BabyGearLab); CR "One-hand fold No". Folds with the toddler seat on if
  it faces forward (help centre 33799203385623).
- **Push:** CR maneuverability 5/5 on a course with grass, root-covered
  trails and curbs. BabyGearLab: "pops up onto curbs", but "struggled more
  with cracks and bumps than other strollers did, possibly due to its
  stiffer suspension". The Bump: sand "almost impossible"; both hands
  needed on rough ground.
- **Canopy:** large, zip-out extension, peekaboo window, drop-down shade
  (CR).
- **Basket:** 30 lb; "easy to access from the front and back, even with
  the seat reclined" (BabyGearLab).
- **Handlebar:** 39.5-42 in (BabyGearLab); stitching uncomfortable on long
  pushes (CR).

### Vista V3

- **Weight and fold:** 27.9 lb (BabyGearLab); "28 pounds, according to our
  lab measurement" (CR). Folded 25.5 × 15.8 × 33.8 in. "When folded, it
  tends to pinch fingers in the folds when carrying or lifting it"
  (BabyGearLab). CR author: "this stroller is just . . . big".
- **Newborn:** the toddler seat is "from 3 months (from birth with optional
  Infant SnugSeat accessory)" (manual). **No hands-on SnugSeat test
  found.**
- **Brakes:** single action, with a window that shows the state
  (BabyGearLab). PureWow: "The pedal lock sometimes gets stuck".
- **Fold:** two hands (BabyGearLab, CR). Help centre 25503522319767:
  brake on, handlebar highest, seat second-most-upright and forward-facing,
  front swivels unlocked, then pull two triggers.
- **Push:** CR maneuverability 5/5. Reviews conflict on rough ground:
  Babylist says it "holds up to just about anything"; CR's author found it
  "rattly, almost rickety" on cobblestones and textured sidewalks.
- **Canopy:** "the best canopy I've ever used on a stroller" (CR author).
- **Basket:** 30 lb. Hard to reach from one side with the toddler seat
  parent-facing (CR).

### Kona (new in 2026; one hands-on write-up)

- **Weight and fold:** maker 22 lb (frame and seat); the bundle page says
  21.2 lb. Folded 13.4 × 21 × 25.8 in (maker); Strolleria gives 10.3 ×
  20.7 × 27.1 in, basis unknown. **No measured figure.** An owner still had
  to take a wheel off to close a 2026 Hyundai Santa Fe trunk (low
  confidence).
- **Newborn:** "For newborns, use the most reclined position of the seat
  and foot barrier is in place" (manual). Babylist: "Newborn-ready seat",
  with no detail on flatness.
- **Fold:** Babylist: "one hand and a few seconds … It's really
  intuitive". Owners disagree: "It gets stuck constantly. It isn't as easy
  as it is advertised"; it sticks with the seat forward-facing (low
  confidence).
- **Push:** "good, but not great … you'll notice the difference over
  bumps" (Babylist).
- **Seat and handlebar:** "The seat sits low, putting your child farther
  from you, and the handlebar wobbles a bit when fully extended. The wobble
  didn't make the stroller feel unstable during my testing" (Babylist).
- **Basket:** 30 lb; "Roomy, accessible" (Babylist).
- No BabyGearLab, The Bump or CR review found.

### Flying (all three)

- Help centre: "The Travel Bag Accessory is recommended for air travel. Our
  TravelSafe program covers any damage incurred to your stroller during air
  travel" (33799154735255, 2026-03-31). TravelSafe needs the stroller to be
  under its original warranty (40280972674583, 2026-10-29).
- Vista/Cruz bag $199.99, with wheels. Kona bag $149.99: "Fits Kona
  stroller with seat attached in either forward or parent-facing
  direction"; "Eliminates the hassle of removing your wheels".
- "No, Kona is not designed to fit in the overhead compartment" (help
  centre).
- No hands-on gate-check or damage report found for any of the three.

### Misuse risks

- Misuse risk: the carrier lock has no visual indicator, only a click and
  a lift check (Aria V2 manual). A click is easy to miss in a noisy place.
- Misuse risk (Kona): no manual describes attaching the carrier to the
  Kona or checking it.
- Misuse risk: UPPAbaby's bundle page says the carrier can face outward on
  the Kona; the Aria V2 manual says parent-facing only.
- Misuse risk: all three manuals say "DO NOT lift the stroller by the
  bumper bar as it may disengage." BabyGearLab praises the Cruz bumper bar
  as "handy for lifting it", and The Bump found no other clear handhold.
- Misuse risk: the Vista V3 seat alone is from 3 months; a newborn needs
  the SnugSeat or the Bassinet. PureWow describes the SnugSeat going in the
  wrong seat, which suggests the options confuse buyers (inference).
- Misuse risk: bags hung on the handlebar can make each stroller unstable
  (all three manuals).

## Confidence

- **High:** Part A document facts, manual warnings, travel bag specs and
  fold rules (current maker documents). "No car seat figure" in the Kona
  manual rests on viewing rendered pages.
- **Medium:** the Kona takes the Aria V2 (every maker web source, no
  manual). Cruz V3 and Vista V3 weight, fold, brakes, canopy and basket
  (BabyGearLab and CR measured and agree; several hands-on reviews of the
  current version).
- **Low:** Vista rough-ground ride (reviews conflict); all Kona usability
  (one editor and a few owners, who disagree on the fold).
- **Not found:** carrier lock, height, stability and basket access for any
  of the three; gate-check damage reports.
