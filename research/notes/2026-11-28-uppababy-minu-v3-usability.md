## Findings

### Weight and fold as used

- Measured 16.7 lb (BabyGearLab), matching the maker. The bumper bar is
  not in the box. **As-used weight with bumper bar and adapters: not
  found.**
- Help centre: "after folding them down with one hand, the adapters will
  fold with the stroller." No measured fold with adapters on.

### Newborn use without the car seat

- Manual: "suitable from birth … Use the most reclined position and the
  rear crotch buckle slot until the baby has strong head and neck control".
- BabyGearLab measured 157.7°, "doesn't go fully flat", and would choose
  the car seat for a newborn. CR: "near-flat recline … safe to use with an
  infant under 6 months". Mumsnet (UK): "I'm not sure I would've used it
  with a tiny newborn myself." Strolleria's "full recline" conflicts with
  the measurement.
- No newborn insert or bassinet is sold for the V3.

### The Aria V2 on the Minu V3: no hands-on evidence

- **Not found:** any hands-on test of an Aria or Mesa on the V3. Lock
  security, lock indication, wobble, carrier height and basket access are
  all unknown. CR lists "Car-seat compatible as sold: No", so its lab
  test likely had no car seat (inference).
- Maker: "Intuitive color-coded dots to indicate proper attachment"
  (adapter to frame); manual: "Audible 'click' must be heard". The Aria
  manual's own check is "Lift up on car seat handle".
- Manual: "When using accessories that attach to stroller handle, always
  have toddler seat in the upright position and infant car seat parent
  facing."
- V2 adapters do not fit the V3, so the 2024 report of a Minu V2 releasing
  a Mesa ([recall note](2026-11-28-stroller-recall-check-method.md)) is
  about a different part.
- Babylist's guide says the seat pairs "directly"; the maker requires
  adapters.

### Brakes

Single-action, colour-coded pedals: "easy to set and release"
(BabyGearLab); "simple, clear, and reliable" (Mumsnet). One owner
(snippet only): "the brake only locked up one of the wheels instead of
both". No source says whether one pedal locks both wheels.

### Fold, carry, airports

| Source | Fold | Carry |
| --- | --- | --- |
| BabyGearLab | "one hand? Maybe for some"; needs a button and a slider at once | padded strap |
| CR | "Very easy, one-handed, self-standing fold" | "strap was too stretchy" |
| Babylist (CPST) | "sometimes takes a few tries" | not comfortable for long carries |
| Fathercraft | one-handed, 8/10 | "You feel those extra pounds hauling through a long terminal" |
| MadeForMums | folding with the seat upright: "alarming crunching noise, and the hood came out of its place" | "Tricky to carry by hand" |

It stands when folded (maker, BabyGearLab, CR).

Gate check: the $99.99 Travel Bag includes TravelSafe, which "covers any
damage to gear during air travel within 4 years". Owners disagree on
whether V3 wheels must come off to fit the bag. One owner (2024, older
Minu) says the bag was sent to the carousel on all five international
flights. **No V3 gate-check damage report found.**

### Push, canopy, basket, handlebar

- Good on pavement and curbs (BabyGearLab; CR maneuverability 5/5). Weak
  on rough ground: "small wheels aren't great on rough terrain" (CR);
  "didn't seem to absorb the shock of … cobbles or gravel"
  (MadeForMums); struggles in thick grass (BabyGearLab). Fathercraft
  disagrees. Hard to push one-handed (Babylist).
- Canopy: "among our favorites", UPF 50+ (BabyGearLab).
- Basket: 20 lb; "our large diaper bag easily fits" (BabyGearLab);
  "somewhat limited" (CR).
- Handlebar: not adjustable (CR); 41 in (BabyGearLab).

### Misuse risks

- Misuse risk: the colour dots show the adapter is on the frame, not that
  the carrier is latched. Lift the carrier every time.
- Misuse risk: a bag hung on the handle can make it unstable (manual),
  more so with a carrier on top (inference).
- Misuse risk: newborn set-up needs the full recline and the rear crotch
  slot; the seat is not fully flat.
- Misuse risk: one owner says the brake held one wheel only (snippet
  only). Check both wheels.
- Misuse risk: used Minu or Minu V2 adapters do not fit the V3.

### What carries over from the V2

Almost nothing: the frame, fold, from-birth seat, bumper bar and
adapters all changed. The travel bag is the same SKU. V2 evidence is low
confidence for the V3.

## Confidence

- **Medium:** push, brakes, canopy, fold, handlebar, basket (several V3
  reviews agree; fold ratings range from "very easy" to "maybe").
- **High:** manual and maker quotes.
- **Low:** gate-check experience, the brake and wheel-locking owner
  reports.
- **Not found:** anything hands-on about the car seat on the V3.
