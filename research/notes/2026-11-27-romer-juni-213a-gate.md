# Romer Juni fails decision 0001: no 213a claim

## Question

Open question 37. A summary Nick shared ranked the Romer Juni above the
Primo Viaggio Nido. The Juni was never in the
[market survey](2026-11-27-infant-car-seat-market-survey.md). Does its maker
claim FMVSS 213a, as [0001](../../decisions/0001-car-seat-must-meet-fmvss-213a.md)
requires? This note also records its survey-table specs, stroller chart,
recalls and Model Y evidence. Usability is out of scope, because the seat
fails the gate.

## Findings

### The 213a claim: none

| Where | Wording | Names 213a? |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Product page (full text and raw HTML searched for "213", "side impact", "FMVSS") | Only FMVSS mention: "*Other textiles and components may be treated in accordance with FMVSS 302 requirements." | No |
| Product page image | "BABY SAFETY ALLIANCE VERIFIED", "Sample-tested to meet U.S. Federal, ASTM and other safety requirements" | No |
| US manual B.2 01.26, p.12 | "This child restraint system conforms to all applicable Federal motor vehicle safety standards." | No |
| 2024 manual (third-party host) | "conforms to all applicable Federal motor vehicle safety standards (FMVSS 213)" | No |
| Versa base manual, FAQ | no standard named | No |

The Juni does not even reach the survey's borderline group, which at least
mentions side impact. It belongs under "No 213a claim found", next to the
Nuna PIPA and Maxi-Cosi seats. The Baby Safety Alliance seal is the same
one flagged on Joie's Rue LX Spin (open question 31).

### Maker and availability

Romer is a Britax Child Safety, Inc. brand (Fort Mill, SC): page footer, and
the manual's trademark line. Romer's store and Strolleria both showed stock
on 2026-11-27.
