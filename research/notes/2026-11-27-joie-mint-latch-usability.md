# Joie Mint Latch: usability and US availability

## Question

Usability pass for the Joie Mint Latch (4-30 lb, under 30 in; rigid LATCH
built into the carrier, so no base is needed; optional Base AR), against
every row of the car-seat "What to judge" list. The weight is on the
rigid-LATCH click-in that happens every trip, and on the baseless belt
install for taxis and aircraft. Also: is it on sale in the US now (open
question 31)?

## Findings

### Version match and manual changes

Every review tested the Mint Latch itself. None states a model number or
manufacture date. Safe in the Seat labels a price "Mint Latch (Meets New
Standards)", which suggests two versions; what changed was not found.

The manual changed between rev F (2024) and rev G (2025-06):

- **Expiry.** Rev F: "expires 7 years from the date of manufacture." Rev G:
  "DO NOT USE THIS CHILD RESTRAINT 7 YEARS AFTER ORIGINAL PURCHASE."
- **Head support.** Rev G adds steps to reattach it. CSFTL found it "way
  too difficult" with rev F, which had none.
- **Harness slip check.** Rev G adds: "pull forward firmly on the harness
  straps at the shoulder. If the straps loosen, clean the harness
  adjustment button and strap", and a cleaning page for it. Inference: Joie
  may have had reports of harness slip. Not verified.
- **Stability test.** Rev F: no more than 1 in side to side. Rev G: "NOT
  MOVE in any direction more than 1 in."

### Rigid LATCH install without a base (the daily click-in)

The manual (rev G): rotate the connectors down, push in, "Listen for two
audible CLICKS. The colors of the indicators on both rigid lower anchor
attachments should be completely green." Then pull on both connectors.
Recline: rotate the connectors until the "LEVEL LINE on the restraint
label is LEVEL WITH THE GROUND." To remove: squeeze the release lever, pull
away, then fold the connectors back to storage.

| Aspect | Finding | Source |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Ease | "relatively easy and painless … takes less than a couple of minutes" (LATCH 9.0/10); "super simple"; CR LATCH fit 5/5 | BabyGearLab; CSFTL; CR |
| Recline | 3 connector angles. "simple, rotating at the carrier connection". **CR con: "Requires re-installing the seat and adjusting recline with every use".** No noodle needed for LATCH. | BabyGearLab; CR |
| Recline indicator | A single blue line on the shell. Accuracy not tested. | CSFTL |
| Stability | "might slide side to side on the LATCH anchors, but this varies by car" (BabyGearLab) vs "hardly moves once it's installed" (CSFTL). They disagree. | as listed |
| Indicators | Green on both connectors means latched. A false green: **not tested by anyone.** | manual |
| Removal | One-handed and quick, but folding the connectors away is a second step they "aren't big fans of"; the connectors "can get stuck". | BabyGearLab |
| Seating positions | Needs dedicated lower anchors, so the center seat "won't work for most vehicles". | CR |
| Durability | On a 2024 unit the release-handle wires "slipped out of their channels"; release still worked; "Joie are working on this issue". No follow-up found. | CSFTL |

- **Misuse risk:** one owner says "one side not latching correctly or
  staying red" at times (Target, 2025-12-22). The indicator caught the
  fault, but one-sided latching happens in daily use. Another owner: "one
  of our latches went bad" (Target, 2026-01-21). Low confidence each.
- **Misuse risk (inference, supported by the CR con):** recline is reset
  by eye on every install, a step a base-mounted seat does not need.

### Baseless belt install (taxis, aircraft)

- Manual: lap belt under both lap belt guides; a towel or pool noodle may
  set recline; "If your vehicle seat belt doesn't lock, use a locking
  clip." **No lock-off on the carrier** (CR, Safe in the Seat). American
  routing only; no European routing (CSFTL).
- Aircraft: "This child restraint is certified for airplane use."
- CSFTL: "clearly marked belt guide that made the seat belt install pretty
  simple."
- BabyGearLab (belt 5.5): simple, but "it wiggles more than we like. Using
  a towel helped". CR: belt 4/5, carrier-only 4/5.
- Safe in the Seat: for international travel, "contact Joie for a locking
  clip". Inference: none in the box; the manual's parts list has none.
- **Misuse risk:** with no lock-off and American routing only, a taxi
  install depends on locking the retractor or fitting a locking clip.

### Base AR (optional)

- Manual rev C: webbing LATCH strap, "Lock-Off Bar", "Anti-Rebound Bar", 4
  recline positions, bubble zones for 0-3 months and 3+ months. "DO NOT
  over tighten … or the lock-off bar will not close." Carrier to base: a
  click plus a pull-up check, no visual indicator.
- **The manual names no tensioner.** The market survey's "belt tensioner"
  came from retail copy ("LockTight belt-tensioner and lock-off").
- Base AR's parts list matches the Rue base's part for part. Inference: it
  is probably the same base, so the
  [Rue note](2026-11-27-joie-rue-usability.md)'s base findings probably
  apply (recline button against the seat back, lock-off band, narrow
  bubble window). Joie does not confirm this.
- No hands-on review of the Mint Latch on Base AR was found.

### Insert and harness fit

- Manual: the insert "MUST be removed once the child reaches 11 lb. (5
  kg)." The head support "can be removed when your child has outgrown it",
  with no criterion.
- CSFTL, 4 lb doll: "With the insert, our doll fit very well." Newborn
  doll: "a bit squished" with the insert, better without.
- Safe in the Seat, 10.5 lb / 23 in 2-month-old: "She had an excellent
  fit!" At 13 months, 19 lb / 28.5 in: "she still fits … quite a bit of
  room above her head".
- 4 and 7 months: not found from CPST or lab sources. Owners conflict: one
  says a 7-month-old was too big (Babylist); one says "already at the top
  shoulder height adjustment" (Target). Low confidence.
- 3 harness heights, rethread; one crotch position (SafetyBeltSafe, CR).
- Head slump: one owner, a baby "in the 5lb range", "the head slump was
  visibly terrible" (Target, 2026-07-22). Low confidence.
- **Misuse risk (low, inference):** rethreading over the back bar instead
  of under it. CSFTL says labels guard against it.

## Confidence

- **High** for the manuals (install steps, indicators, recline, 11 lb
  insert, expiry by revision, no lock-off, Base AR hardware).
- **Medium** for rigid-LATCH ease, the recline-every-trip step, removal
  friction, harness-release friction and canopy: BabyGearLab, CSFTL and CR
  agree. CSFTL's unit predates manual rev G.
- **Low to medium** for LATCH stability (reviewers disagree); **medium-low**
  for belt-install stability.
- **Low** for indicator reliability, fit at 4 and 7 months, and Base AR use.
- **Medium** for availability (retailers; Joie's site blocked).
