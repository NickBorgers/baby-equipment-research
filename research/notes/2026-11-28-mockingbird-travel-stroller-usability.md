## Findings

### Price

$349 Black Friday sale, $399 regular, seen 2026-11-28. In the box:
"Travel Stroller, FlexConnect™ Car Seat Adapter, Cup Holder, Carry Bag".
QuickConnect click-in adapter $50. Black showed "Sold out"; other colours
not checked. "standard 30-day try-at-home"; 60 days for Black Friday
orders. Warranty: "lifetime warranty, with a 2-year warranty on included
accessories". Shipping began mid-October 2026.

### What it is (maker figures; nothing measured)

- 17.7 lb (17.6 lb in the page's own table). As-used weight: not found.
- Open: "18" wide, 31" from handlebar to front wheels, 41.5" handlebar
  height". Handlebar fixed (Stroller Mom).
- Basket: "Maximum weight is 20lbs (10kg)" (manual).
- Wheels: 5 in front, 6 in rear, airless, all-wheel suspension (Stroller
  Mom's spec table).

### Newborn use without the car seat

- Manual: "Use the newborn nest mode or most reclined position until the
  baby has strong head and neck control and can sit unassisted." Setup:
  fully recline, raise the leg rest, snap and button the Nest Mode shade
  so it sits "snugly … with no sagging or gaps".
- Manual: "Never use in the reclined carriage position unless the
  enclosure is raised to cover the front opening." "The stroller cannot
  be folded while in Infant Nest Mode."
- Help: the Mockingbird Bassinet and Infant Seat Insert "aren't
  compatible with it".
- Stroller Mom (CPST): "it is so flat". She did not use it with a newborn.
  No source measured the recline.

### The Aria V2 on the FlexConnect strap adapter

From the FlexConnect manual (2026-00736):

- Prepare: "Engage the brake and ensure the harness is fully fastened.
  Recline the seat fully and raise the leg rest until it is parallel with
  the seat."
- Adapters: "Slide the adapters down until they lock into place on both
  sides. Do not attach the adapters to the Infant Nest Mode round peg above
  the tray track. You will hear a 'CLICK' when each side is secure."
- Seat: "Place the infant car seat facing the stroller handlebars on the
  stroller seat base … loop the long strap up toward the back of the
  stroller seat and around the car seat handle hub … Make sure the strap
  stays flat and untwisted … Pull up on the ring at the end of the strap
  to fully tighten. … Pull up on the car seat handle to ensure the car
  seat is secured to the stroller."
- Warnings: "Straps can wrap around a child's neck." "Always remove the
  FlexConnect car seat adapter from the stroller when not in use." "do not
  lift or carry the stroller using the car seat."
- No weight limit for the adapter; it defers to the car seat manual.
- The seat list names "UPPAbaby Aria v1 or Aria v2". The maker says both
  "40+" and "50+" compatible seats on different pages.

How you know it is held: the adapter clicks into the frame. The strap has
only the pull-up check, with no tension indicator.

Reviewers:

- Stroller Mom: "It is not as smooth as peg adapters, per se, but it works
  well with many infant car seats." Seat not named.
- Forbes: tested "on an Evenflo infant car seat … hold the seat securely".
  No Evenflo seat is on Mockingbird's list.
- What to Expect describes clicking the seat "directly onto the frame",
  which does not match the strap design. Given little weight.
- **No reviewer used an UPPAbaby seat.** Stability with a carrier and
  carrier height: not found. The carrier rests on the seat base, so it
  likely sits at seat height (inference).
- The QuickConnect manual was not read.

### Brakes

Single pedal; manual: "press down on the pedal until it clicks. Push the
stroller forward and pull it back to verify". "simple and flip-flop
friendly" (Stroller Mom). The Everymom: "A little bit of gravity can start
pulling the stroller when we stop on an incline … So if I'm stopped, I
lock it."

### Fold and carry

Manual: "press the handlebar down to trigger the autofold … It can also
stand upright on its own." Babylist: "folds in a few seconds with just one
hand … self-standing". Carry strap on the seat; Stroller Mom "definitely
noticed the weight if I had it on my shoulder for a while". The bumper bar
must fold down first.

### Flying

Stroller Mom carried it into an Air France overhead bin after the crew
first refused. She recommends a padded bag even so. The included carry bag
is for gate-checking. No Delta, American, gate-check or long-term
durability report found.

### Push, canopy, basket

- Stroller Mom: "cobblestones, gravel, pavement, airport floors, sidewalks,
  and uneven city streets, and it did really well on all of them. I would
  not take it on rough trails all the time". Babylist: "the all-wheel
  suspension shines over rougher terrain".
- Forbes: "does jerk to stop when I unexpectedly hit an uneven slab";
  a leaning 4-year-old could pitch the seat forward.
- Canopy: "really good coverage", mesh window (Stroller Mom). Basket:
  "roomy and easy to access" (Babylist). Basket access with a carrier on:
  not found.

### Misuse risks

- Misuse risk: the FlexConnect strap is tightened by hand with no tension
  indicator; a loose, twisted or mis-routed strap gives no cue. The pull-up
  check is the only test.
- Misuse risk: a second, wrong peg (the Nest Mode peg) sits near the right
  one; the manual warns against it.
- Misuse risk: setup depends on the stroller's state (full recline, leg
  rest up, brake on, harness fastened), and nothing enforces it
  (inference from the manual).
- Misuse risk: straps left on are a strangulation hazard; the manual says
  to remove them.
- Misuse risk: the straps physically hold unlisted seats (Forbes used one).
- Misuse risk: Nest Mode needs the enclosure fully fastened, or the
  leg-opening strangulation warning applies.

## Confidence

- **High:** price and manual wording (primary, read word for word).

- **Not found:** measured weight or fold, carrier height, stability with a
