# Title

## Question

State it narrowly. One note, one question. If the research sprawled, split it.

## Sources

Every source gets a URL and the date it was read. Say what kind each one is —
they are not equal:

- **primary** — NHTSA, CPSC, a published standard, the manufacturer's own
  manual or spec sheet
- **independent testing** — Consumer Reports, IIHS-style lab testing, a
  reviewer who measured something themselves
- **retail / anecdotal** — a retailer listing, user reviews, a forum or
  Reddit thread, a chat summary

- [Title of the page](https://example.com) — primary, accessed YYYY-MM-DD

## Findings

What the sources actually say. Keep numbers with their units and with where
they came from — a manufacturer's "folds to 22 in" is not the same claim as a
reviewer's tape measure.

## Confidence

Say why the confidence is what it is:

- **high** — primary source (regulator, standard, manufacturer manual), or
  measured here
- **medium** — independent testing, or several retail/anecdotal sources that
  agree
- **low** — one review or forum post, an inference, or a figure for a
  different model year or variant

Low confidence is fine to record. Low confidence presented as fact is not.
