# 0004 — Buy the UPPAbaby Aria V2 infant car seat

## Context

Decision [0002](0002-infant-car-seat-that-clicks-onto-the-stroller.md)
chose an infant car seat that clicks onto the stroller. Decision
[0001](0001-car-seat-must-meet-fmvss-213a.md) requires a 213a claim, and
[0003](0003-no-rotation-now-buy-for-the-long-run.md) rules out a rotating
infant seat. Usability research left three seats on the shortlist: the
[UPPAbaby Aria V2](../docs/products/uppababy-aria-v2.md), the
[UPPAbaby Mesa V3](../docs/products/uppababy-mesa-v3.md) and the
[Chicco KeyFit 30 ClearTex](../docs/products/chicco-keyfit-30-cleartex.md).

How we will use it (Nick, 2026-11-27 and 2026-11-28):

- The base stays installed in our 2022 Model Y, in an outboard rear seat,
  on the lower LATCH anchors.
- Installs without the base are for travel only: taxis, rental cars and
  other people's cars.
- We will fly with it from the start. On aircraft, all three seats use a
  lap-belt-only method without the base
  ([FAA note](../research/notes/2026-11-27-faa-car-seat-use-on-aircraft.md), [Aria V2 note](../research/notes/2026-11-28-uppababy-aria-v2-carrier-only-install.md)).

Nick chose the Aria V2 on 2026-11-28, after a devil's-advocate review
argued the case against it.

## Decision

We buy the UPPAbaby Aria V2 (1002-ARI, US), with its base installed on
LATCH in the Model Y. Before the return window closes, we test-install it
in our car (see Consequences). If the base cannot reach the right recline
or pass the 1-inch test there, we fall back to the Mesa V3.

## Rationale

**1. The load leg, on the install we use every day.** The Aria V2 base has
a load leg and an anti-rebound panel. The Mesa V3 and KeyFit 30 bases have
neither ([survey](../research/notes/2026-11-27-infant-car-seat-market-survey.md)). A [peer-reviewed sled study](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC8535498/) found a support leg "was associated
with significant reductions of head injury metrics below injury tolerance
limits for all tests"
([load-leg note](../research/notes/2026-11-28-load-leg-sled-test-evidence.md)).
Our Model Y floor is solid, so the leg has a floor to stand on
([Model Y note](../research/notes/2026-11-27-model-y-infant-seat-fit-and-load-leg.md)).

**2. LATCH avoids the Aria's weakest install.** [BabyGearLab](https://www.babygearlab.com/reviews/vehicle-safety/infant-car-seat/uppababy-aria-v2) found
the belt could slip in the Aria V2's lock-off. Our base goes in on LATCH,
which [BabyGearLab](https://www.babygearlab.com/reviews/vehicle-safety/infant-car-seat/uppababy-aria-v2) called "easy" and
[Consumer Reports](https://www.consumerreports.org/babies-kids/car-seats/uppababy-aria/m415804/) scored 5/5 on the V1
([usability note](../research/notes/2026-11-27-uppababy-aria-v2-usability.md),
[CR note](../research/notes/2026-11-28-consumer-reports-infant-seats.md)).
By contrast, the Mesa V3's LATCH install moved "about 1 inch side to side
with effort" in [BabyGearLab's test](https://www.babygearlab.com/reviews/vehicle-safety/infant-car-seat/uppababy-mesa-v3), at the manual's limit
([Mesa V3 note](../research/notes/2026-11-27-uppababy-mesa-v3-usability.md)).

**3. The lightest carrier, for travel.** [About 7 lb as used](../research/notes/2026-11-27-uppababy-aria-v2-usability.md),
against [10.9 lb measured](https://www.babygearlab.com/reviews/vehicle-safety/infant-car-seat/uppababy-mesa-v3) for the Mesa V3 and
[about 9-9.5 lb](../research/notes/2026-11-27-chicco-keyfit-30-usability.md) for the KeyFit 30. We will carry it through
airports and down plane aisles from the start.

**4. It keeps the stroller choice the same as the Mesa.** Both UPPAbaby
seats fit the same strollers: Vista and Cruz directly, Minu, Minu Duo and
Ridge with adapters, plus Mockingbird with adapters
([stroller note](../research/notes/2026-11-28-uppababy-aria-v2-on-stroller-and-model-y.md)).

**What the devil's-advocate review corrected.** Two reasons given earlier
([open question 38](../research/open-questions.md)) were weaker than stated:

- A stable install without the base is **not** an Aria advantage.
  [BabyGearLab](https://www.babygearlab.com/reviews/vehicle-safety/infant-car-seat/uppababy-mesa-v3) found the Mesa V3 just as stable that way, with no
  towel.
- The Aria V2 allows only one way to install without the base in a car
  ([note](../research/notes/2026-11-28-uppababy-aria-v2-carrier-only-install.md)).
  This is a cost, not a strength. See Consequences.

So the choice rests on reasons 1-4 above, not on the baseless install.

## Alternatives considered

- **UPPAbaby Mesa V3.** It keeps [three baseless methods](../research/notes/2026-11-27-uppababy-mesa-v3-usability.md), has a
  32 in
  height limit instead of 30 in, a smoother harness tightener, and costs
  $50 less. It lost because it has no load leg, its LATCH install was the
  least steady of the three in testing, and its carrier is about 4 lb
  heavier. [Consumer Reports](https://www.consumerreports.org/babies-kids/car-seats/uppababy-mesa-v2/m408511/) did not recommend the Mesa V2 (68,
  24th of 46); the V3 is [untested](../research/notes/2026-11-28-consumer-reports-infant-seats.md). It stays the fallback if the Aria fails the
  test install.
- **Chicco KeyFit 30 ClearTex.** It has the strongest outside evidence:
  [Consumer Reports](https://www.consumerreports.org/babies-kids/car-seats/chicco-keyfit-30/m90195/) 86 (6th of 46, version not stated), the only
  Model Y test ([Cars.com](https://www.cars.com/articles/how-do-car-seats-fit-in-a-2021-tesla-model-y-436721/), grade A), the lowest price and the widest stroller
  range. It lost because it has no load leg, allows no towel under the
  base if the bubble cannot reach the range, is "wobbly" without the base
  with no European routing, needs a rethread harness, and expires after 6
  years ([KeyFit 30 note](../research/notes/2026-11-27-chicco-keyfit-30-usability.md)).
- **Peg Perego Primo Viaggio Nido.** It has a load leg, but its recline
  may be out of reach in some cars with no fix allowed under the base, its
  click-in indicator showed green while unlatched
  [in 2017](https://csftl.org/peg-perego-primo-viaggio-4-35-nido-review/), and no review
  covers the current 4-30 version
  ([4-30 note](../research/notes/2026-11-28-primo-viaggio-nido-4-30-fixes.md)).
- **Wait for [Safe in the Seat](https://safeintheseat.com/seats/uppababy-aria-v2)'s V2 review.** It is "coming
  soon" ([note](../research/notes/2026-11-28-uppababy-aria-v2-hands-on-and-fit-update.md)). We chose
  not to wait; the test install below covers the risks a review would.

## Consequences

**Checks we commit to, before the return window closes:**

1. Test-install the base in our Model Y on LATCH. Remove the floor mat
   under the load leg. Check: the recline bubble is fully between the
   lines without a towel; the load leg reaches the floor with its
   indicator green; the base moves less than 1 in at the belt path after
   the indicator turns green; and front legroom.
2. Drive, then repeat the 1-inch test. [Owners](../research/notes/2026-11-28-uppababy-aria-v2-hands-on-and-fit-update.md) of
   [both UPPAbaby bases](../research/notes/2026-11-28-infant-seats-firecrawl-retry.md) report the indicator falling back to red
   after driving ([open question 42](../research/open-questions.md)).
3. Every time: never trust the green tightness indicator alone; do the
   1-inch test after any reinstall. Lift the carrier after clicking it
   onto the base or the stroller.

**What it commits us to:**

- The stroller must accept the Aria V2: an UPPAbaby stroller, or one with
  [Mockingbird adapters](../research/notes/2026-11-28-uppababy-aria-v2-on-stroller-and-model-y.md). This narrows the stroller search under
  [0003](0003-no-rotation-now-buy-for-the-long-run.md).
- Without the base, only European routing is allowed
  ([note](../research/notes/2026-11-28-uppababy-aria-v2-carrier-only-install.md)). A car seat with a lap-only belt cannot take the carrier
  without its base, and inflatable vehicle belts are banned for every
  install. When travelling, bring the
  base, or check the car's belts first.
- The [height limit is 30 in](../research/notes/2026-11-27-infant-car-seat-market-survey.md). Babies may outgrow it sooner than
  the Mesa V3's 32 in (low confidence). The next seat, a convertible under
  [0003](0003-no-rotation-now-buy-for-the-long-run.md),
  may be needed earlier.
- Cost: $399.99 for the seat and base, $229.99 for an extra base (prices
  seen during Black Friday 2026; [survey](../research/notes/2026-11-27-infant-car-seat-market-survey.md)).

  evidence is V1 or incentivized owner reviews.
  [Consumer Reports](../research/notes/2026-11-28-consumer-reports-infant-seats.md) has tested only the V1
  ([open question 40](../research/open-questions.md)).
- **Model Y recline and front legroom:** not researched by anyone; the
  test install covers it ([open question 30](../research/open-questions.md)).
- **Insert and harness fit at 4, 7 and 9-12 months:**
  [not found](../research/notes/2026-11-28-uppababy-aria-v2-hands-on-and-fit-update.md).
- **Carrier height on the stroller:** [not found](../research/notes/2026-11-28-infant-seats-firecrawl-retry.md)
  ([open question 28](../research/open-questions.md)).
- **Headrest height against the Mesa V3:** [not measured](../research/notes/2026-11-28-uppababy-aria-v2-hands-on-and-fit-update.md).
- **What UPPAbaby advises when a shoulder belt will not wrap the shell:**
  not asked ([note](../research/notes/2026-11-28-uppababy-aria-v2-carrier-only-install.md), [open question 39](../research/open-questions.md)).
- **Whether the base loosens or the indicator misreads after driving:**
  unknown ([open question 42](../research/open-questions.md)).
- **Durability:** single [owner reports](../research/notes/2026-11-28-uppababy-aria-v2-hands-on-and-fit-update.md) only.
- **Older-version evidence:** the ["challenging to level"](../research/notes/2026-11-27-uppababy-aria-v2-usability.md)
  finding, the [CR scores](https://www.consumerreports.org/babies-kids/car-seats/uppababy-aria/m415804/) and the
